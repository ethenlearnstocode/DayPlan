How to Use DayPlan

STEP 1
Select a Day & Enter Task
Locate the day column (Monday–Sunday) and type your task description into the input box.
STEP 2
Set Start & Finish Times
Pick your planned start time and end time to block out your schedule.
STEP 3
Tick Off Wins (✓)
When you finish a task, click the green tick button. It marks the card green and displays a celebratory message!
STEP 4
Mark Missed Tasks (✕)
If you couldn't complete a task, click the red cross button. Type why it failed in the popup, and DayPlan logs your reflection.

How to Use DayPlan

STEP 1
Add to Home Screen
Open your website link in Safari on iOS. Tap Share and select Add to Home Screen.
STEP 2
Enable Phone Alerts
Tap 🔔 Enable Phone Alerts at the top header and press "Allow" when prompted.
STEP 3
Edit Tasks Instantly
Tap the ✏️ pencil icon on any task to adjust its title or start/end times without having to delete it.
